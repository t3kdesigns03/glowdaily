# Pop-Ups & Events — Jennifer's Runbook

Plain-English guide to running a pop-up, tea drop, or school stop with online pre-orders.
No tech knowledge needed.

---

## The big idea

People **order and pay online ahead of time**, then **pick up at your stop**. You get every
order in your dashboard, sorted by event, so you can pack the whole batch before you go.

You do three things: **add the event → share the link → pack the orders.**

---

## 1. Add an event (once per stop)

1. Open your **Dashboard** and sign in.
2. Tap the **📍 Events** tab.
3. Tap **+ New Event** and fill in:
   - **Name** — e.g. "Lincoln Elementary Fall Kickoff"
   - **Type** — Pop-Up, Tea Drop, or School
   - **Date**, **Start / End time**
   - **Location** — where they pick up, e.g. "Gym entrance"
   - **Notes** (optional) — e.g. "Order by Friday noon"
   - Leave **Active** checked so people can order.
4. The **link slug** fills in automatically from the name (you can edit it). This becomes
   the web link.
5. Tap **Add Event**. Done — it's instantly live on your site.

> The event now shows on your public calendar at **glowdailynutrition.com/popups/** and
> gets its own order link.

---

## 2. Share the link

On the event's card in the Events tab, tap **🔗 Copy link**. You'll get something like:

```
https://glowdailynutrition.com/e/?event=lincoln-elem-fall
```

Paste that into the class group chat, the PTA email, a flyer QR code, your Instagram bio —
anywhere. When someone opens it on their phone, the site already knows it's for **that
event**: they just build their order and check out. They can't accidentally pick shipping.

You can also just send people to **glowdailynutrition.com/popups/** to see everything at once.

---

## 3. Pack the orders (day of, or the night before)

1. Dashboard → **📦 Orders** tab.
2. Tap the **Event** filter chip, then pick the school/stop from the dropdown.
3. You now see **only that event's orders**. Each row shows the customer, items, and 📍 the
   event name. Tap a row to see flavors, custom Flavor Friday notes, and pack details.
4. Pack the batch. Mark each **Received → Packed** as you go.

That's the whole job.

---

## 4. Payments (unchanged)

Same as always — the customer picks at checkout:
- **Venmo** `@GlowDaily` (most orders)
- **Square** (you send a link)
- **Cash** at pickup

The site never charges a card; you confirm and collect like you do today.

---

## 5. Turning an event off

- **Done with a stop?** Open it in the Events tab and flip **Active → off** (or Delete it).
  It immediately disappears from the calendar and the order dropdowns. Orders already placed
  stay safe in your dashboard.
- Events also turn themselves off automatically **after their date passes.**

---

## 6. "Other / Tea Drop" — for one-offs

If someone orders from your **main site** (not an event link) and picks **Event / Pop-Up →
Other / Tea Drop**, they type where they want it (e.g. "Sunday tea drop at Salon X"). That
shows up on the order too, so you still know where it's going — even without a formal event.

---

## Quick reference

| I want to… | Where |
|---|---|
| Add / edit / turn off an event | Dashboard → **Events** tab |
| Get a shareable link | Event card → **🔗 Copy link** |
| See the public calendar | **glowdailynutrition.com/popups/** |
| Pack one school's orders | Dashboard → **Orders** → Event filter → pick the event |
| A stop's order page | **glowdailynutrition.com/e/?event=SLUG** |

**Nothing shows on the public pages until you add an event.** That's normal — the calendar
is empty on purpose until you put a stop on it.
